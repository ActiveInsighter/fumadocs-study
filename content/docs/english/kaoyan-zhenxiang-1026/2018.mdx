## Section I 完形填空：信任、催产素与辨别欺骗

### 全文逻辑结构

<Steps>
  <Step>

  ### 提出矛盾：信任必要，但也可能付出代价

  作者通过 `On the one hand` 与 `On the other hand` 强调信任的两面性，并提出“为什么仍然选择信任”。

</Step>
  <Step>

  ### 生理解释：信任的愉悦感与催产素

  信任可能引发催产素释放；研究者通过受试者对陌生人的借款行为展示更强的信任倾向。

</Step>
  <Step>

  ### 行为解释：识别不可靠的人

  研究以幼儿是否遭到测试员欺骗为条件，比较他们后续参与合作活动的意愿。

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="避免把完形文章误读成科学定论">

文章报告的是特定研究情境中的观察结果：催产素与信任行为有关，幼儿也表现出区分可靠与不可靠成人的能力。**这不等于**所有人都必然因催产素而盲目信任，也不能把某个测试结果泛化为普遍因果法则。

</Callout>

### 第一段：信任的两面性

**① Trust is a tricky business.**

译：信任是一件复杂而棘手的事。

- `trust` 为不可数抽象名词，在此作主语。
- `business` 在这里不是“商业”，而是“一件事”；`tricky` 表“难以处理、复杂的”。

**② On the one hand, it's a necessary condition for many worthwhile things: child care, friendships, etc.**

译：一方面，信任是很多值得投入的事情所必需的条件，例如照顾孩子、维系友谊等。

- `On the one hand` 与后句的 `On the other hand` 构成全文的**两面论证**。
- `a necessary condition for ...` = “……的必要条件”；冒号后列出实例，并非另起独立观点。

**③ On the other hand, putting your faith in the wrong place often carries a high price.**

译：另一方面，把信任交给错误的人或对象，往往要付出沉重代价。

- **主语：** `putting your faith in the wrong place` 是动名词短语，整个结构作单数主语。
- `put one's faith in ...` = “信任……、寄希望于……”；`carry a high price` = “代价高昂”。
- `in the wrong place` 是比喻用法，“信任错付”，不一定是物理地点。

### 第二段：信任的生理机制与瑞士实验

**① Then, why do we trust at all?**

译：那么，我们究竟为什么还会相信别人呢？

- `at all` 在疑问句中加强语气，可译为“究竟、到底”；`Then` 承接上文的矛盾。

**② Well, because it feels good.**

译：因为信任让人感觉愉悦。

- 回答上一问的省略句，`because` 引出原因，不必硬译为不完整句。

**③ When people place their trust in an individual or an institution, their brains release oxytocin, a hormone that produces pleasurable feelings and triggers the herding instinct that prompts humans to connect with one another.**

译：当人们信任某个人或机构时，大脑会释放催产素。这种激素能带来愉悦感，并激发使人们彼此联系的群居本能。

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="长难句：when → a hormone that → instinct that">

  **主干：** `their brains / release / oxytocin`。

  **逐层分析：**

  1. `When people place their trust in ...`：时间状语从句。
  2. `a hormone`：同位语，解释 `oxytocin`（催产素）。
  3. `that produces pleasurable feelings and triggers the herding instinct`：`that` 引导定语从句修饰 `hormone`，其中 `produces` 与 `triggers` 并列。
  4. `that prompts humans to connect with one another`：第二个 `that` 从句修饰 `instinct`，并不是与第一个 `that` 平行。

  **搭配：** `prompt sb. to do sth.` 促使某人做某事；`connect with one another` 与彼此建立联系。

</Accordion>
</Accordions>

**④ Scientists have found that exposure to this hormone puts us in a trusting mood: In a Swiss study, researchers sprayed oxytocin into the noses of half the subjects; those subjects were ready to lend significantly higher amounts of money to strangers than were their counterparts who inhaled something else.**

译：科学家发现，接触这种激素会使人更愿意信任他人：在一项瑞士研究中，研究者让一半受试者通过鼻腔吸入催产素；这些受试者愿意借给陌生人的金额，明显高于吸入其他物质的对照组。

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="长难句：冒号解释 + 分号并列 + than 倒装">

  **前半句：** `Scientists have found [that exposure to this hormone puts us in a trusting mood]`。`exposure to` 为“接触某物”；`put sb. in a ... mood` 是“使某人处于某种情绪/心境”。

  **冒号之后：** 瑞士研究是用来**说明前文发现**的例子。分号连接同一研究中的两项事实：研究如何操作、受试者如何表现。

  **比较主干：** `those subjects / were ready to lend / significantly higher amounts of money ... / than were their counterparts`。

  `than were their counterparts` 发生了**主谓倒装**，还省略了被比较的谓语内容；还原为：`than their counterparts were [ready to lend]`。

  **定语从句：** `who inhaled something else` 修饰 `their counterparts`，表示对照组。

  <Callout type="warn" title="比较对象要保持一致">

  被比较的不是“受试者人数”，而是两组人**愿意出借的金额**。译文宜补出“高于……愿意出借的金额”，避免读成“借钱人数更多”。

</Callout>

</Accordion>
</Accordions>

### 第三段：幼儿识别欺骗的实验设计

**① Lucky for us, we also have a sixth sense for dishonesty that may protect us.**

译：幸运的是，我们似乎还具有辨别不诚实行为的某种直觉，而这种直觉可能保护我们。

- `Lucky for us` = “对我们来说幸运的是”；`a sixth sense` 是“第六感、直觉”。
- `that may protect us` 主要修饰 `a sixth sense for dishonesty`；`may` 表可能性。

**② A Canadian study found that children as young as 14 months can differentiate between a credible person and a dishonest one.**

译：加拿大的一项研究发现，甚至只有十四个月大的幼儿也能够区分可信的人与不诚实的人。

- `children as young as 14 months`：甚至年仅十四个月的孩子，`as ... as` 突出年龄小。
- `differentiate between A and B` 区分 A 和 B；`one` 代替前文的 `person`。

**③ Sixty toddlers were each introduced to an adult tester holding a plastic container.**

译：六十名学步幼儿分别被引见给一位手持塑料容器的成年测试员。

- 被动结构 `were introduced to`；`each` 强调每个孩子分别参与。
- `holding a plastic container` 是现在分词短语，修饰 `tester`，不是幼儿手持容器。

**④ The tester would ask, “What's in here?” before looking into the container, smiling, and exclaiming, “Wow!”**

译：测试员会先问“这里面是什么？”，随后看向容器内部，露出笑容并惊呼“哇！”。

- `would ask` 描述实验中反复采用的流程，不是单纯过去将来时。
- `before` 后三个并列动名词动作：`looking`、`smiling`、`exclaiming`。

**⑤ Each subject was then invited to look inside.**

译：接着，每个参与测试的孩子都被请去查看容器里面。

- `be invited to do` 被邀请做……；`subject` 此处指**实验参与者**，不是学科科目。

**⑥ Half of them found a toy; the other half discovered the container was empty—and realized the tester had fooled them.**

译：一半孩子发现里面有个玩具；另一半却发现容器是空的，并意识到测试员骗了他们。

- `Half ...; the other half ...` 通过分号实现对照；`discovered [that] the container was empty` 省略宾语从句的 `that`。
- `had fooled` 过去完成时，表欺骗早于孩子们“意识到”这一动作。

### 第四段：不同条件下的合作行为

**① Among the children who had not been tricked, the majority were willing to cooperate with the tester in learning a new skill, demonstrating that they trusted his leadership.**

译：在没有被骗过的孩子中，大多数愿意与测试员合作学习新技能，这表明他们信任测试员的引导。

- **主干：** `the majority / were / willing to cooperate`；`among ...` 限定统计人群。
- `who had not been tricked` 是定语从句，修饰 `children`。
- `demonstrating that they trusted his leadership` 是现在分词短语，对前面的合作行为进行解释；其中 `that ...` 是 `demonstrating` 的宾语从句。

**② In contrast, only five of the 30 children paired with the “unreliable” tester participated in a follow-up activity.**

译：相比之下，与“不可靠”的测试员配对的三十名孩子中，仅有五名参加了后续活动。

- `In contrast` 明确提示组间对比；`only five of the 30 children` 的分母是该组 **30 名幼儿**。
- `paired with ...` 为过去分词后置定语，修饰 `children`；`follow-up` 指“后续的”。
- 这句话提示信任经历会影响后续合作意愿，但不构成所有环境下都如此的必然规律。

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="完形逻辑训练：为什么 on the other hand 与 in contrast 不可互换？">

  - `on the one hand ... on the other hand ...`：用于**同一主题的不同方面**，本文谈信任的必要性和风险。
  - `in contrast`：用于**两个对象或实验组的结果对照**，本文比较两组幼儿的后续合作行为。
  - `then`：承接前文提出待回答的问题；`because`：作直接回答。

  复习时先辨明段落功能，再考虑词语选择，而不是仅比较中文词义是否相近。

</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Reading Text 1：自动化与中产阶级就业

<Callout type="info" title="核心论证链">

**自动化冲击并非平均分配。**&#x2060;文章指出中产阶级部分工作容易被技术替代，富人可能因拥有机器资本而受益；历史经验虽说明技术长期能创造就业，但中期转型仍会带来压力。因此作者提出教育改革、扶持企业创新、调整税收和收入支持政策三类应对方案。文中的研究比例与政策观点属于**文章所述时代的论证材料**，不应直接当作当前就业数据。

</Callout>

### Text 1 · 逐句精读与语法重点

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="第一段｜地点状语前置倒装与设问">

  **原句：** Among the annoying challenges facing the middle class is one that will probably go unmentioned in the next presidential campaign: What happens when the robots come for their jobs?

  **顺序还原：** `One that will probably go unmentioned ... is among the annoying challenges ...`。

  - `Among the annoying challenges ...` 位于句首，句子采用**全部倒装**：介词短语 + 系动词 `is` + 主语 `one`。
  - `facing the middle class` 修饰 `challenges`，意思是“中产阶级所面临的”。
  - `that will probably go unmentioned ...` 修饰 `one`；`go unmentioned` = “未被提及”。
  - 冒号后 `What happens ...?` 是对 `one` 具体内容的解释；`when` 为时间状语从句。
  - `come for their jobs` 不只是“来到工作场所”，而是语境中的**争夺/取代他们的工作**。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="第二段｜at high risk + with 独立主格 + 省略">

  **① About half of U.S. jobs are at high risk of being automated, according to a University of Oxford study, with the middle class disproportionately squeezed.**

  - **主干：** `About half of U.S. jobs / are / at high risk`。
  - `of being automated`：`of` 后用动名词的**被动式**；“面临被自动化替代的高风险”。
  - `according to ...` 是说明论据来源的插入成分。
  - `with the middle class disproportionately squeezed`：`with + 名词 + 过去分词` 的复合结构，说明伴随结果；不是另一个带限定谓语的独立句。

  **② But many middle-class occupations—trucking, financial advice, software engineering—have aroused their interest, or soon will.**

  - 破折号内是中产阶级职业举例；`their` 回指**robots**（拟人化）。
  - `or soon will` 省略 `arouse their interest`；表示“已经引起或即将引起”。
  - 前面的 `Lower-income jobs ... don't appeal to robots` 与这一句构成对照，不能断言所有低薪工作都不会被自动化。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="第三段｜历史类比、whose 定语与比较句">

  **① The Industrial Revolution didn't go so well for Luddites whose jobs were displaced by mechanized looms, but it eventually raised living standards and created more jobs than it destroyed.**

  - **分句 A：** `The Industrial Revolution / didn't go so well / for Luddites`。
  - `whose jobs were displaced by mechanized looms` 是定语从句，修饰 `Luddites`；`whose` 指“他们的工作”。
  - **分句 B：** `it / raised / living standards`，并列 `created more jobs than it destroyed`。
  - `than it destroyed` 中的比较对象都是 **jobs**，后半省略可从语境还原的 `jobs`。

  **② Likewise, automation should eventually boost productivity, stimulate demand by driving down prices, and free workers from hard, boring work.**

  - 三个谓语并列，均受情态动词 `should` 支配：`boost`、`stimulate`、`free`。
  - `by driving down prices` 表刺激需求的手段；`drive down` = “压低”。
  - `free sb. from sth.` = “使某人摆脱某事”。

  **③ But in the medium term, middle-class workers may need a lot of help adjusting.**

  - `in the medium term` = 中期、过渡阶段；与 `eventually` 的长期前景对照。
  - `help adjusting` 可理解为 `help [with/in] adjusting`，`adjusting` 表明具体需要支持的方面。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="第四段｜教育改革：as 插入、less ... more ...、宾补">

  **① The first step, as Erik Brynjolfsson and Andrew McAfee argue in The Second Machine Age, should be rethinking education and job training.**

  - **主干：** `The first step / should be / rethinking education and job training`。
  - `as ... argue ...` 为插入性的补充评论从句，可理解为“正如……所说”；不能把 `argue` 错当主句谓语。
  - `rethinking ...` 是动名词短语作表语，强调“重新审视”。

  **② Curriculums—from grammar school to college—should evolve to focus less on memorizing facts and more on creativity and complex communication.**

  - **主干：** `Curriculums / should evolve / to focus ...`；插入语说明适用学段。
  - `less on A and more on B` 是对称比较结构：减少侧重 A、增加侧重 B。
  - `memorizing facts` 是介词 `on` 的宾语；不要把 `more on creativity` 当作另一个新的谓语。

  **③ Vocational schools should do a better job of fostering problem-solving skills and helping students work alongside robots.**

  - `do a better job of doing sth.` = 更好地做好某事。
  - `of` 后并列两个动名词：`fostering ...` 和 `helping ...`。
  - `help students [to] work alongside robots`：`to` 可省略；`alongside` 表“与……协同”。

  **④ It could make extra training and instruction affordable.**

  - `make + 宾语 + 形容词宾补`：`extra training and instruction` 是宾语，`affordable` 是宾补。
  - `It` 回指 `Online education`。

  **⑤ Professionals trying to acquire new skills will be able to do so without going into debt.**

  - `trying to acquire new skills` 后置修饰 `Professionals`。
  - `do so` 代替前面的 `acquire new skills`，避免重复；`without going into debt` 说明不必负债。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="第五段｜the need for sb. to do 与创业转型">

  **① The challenge of coping with automation underlines the need for the U.S. to revive its fading business dynamism: Starting new companies must be made easier.**

  - **主干：** `The challenge ... / underlines / the need ...`。
  - `of coping with automation` 修饰 `challenge`；`the need for the U.S. to revive ...` 是“美国有必要重振……”。
  - `for the U.S. to revive ...` 是带**逻辑主语**的不定式结构，说明 `need` 的具体内容。
  - 冒号后给出政策方向：`Starting new companies` 是动名词短语作主语；`must be made easier` 是被动的 `make + 宾语 + 形容词宾补` 结构。

  **② In previous eras of drastic technological change, entrepreneurs smoothed the transition by dreaming up ways to combine labor and machines.**

  - **主干：** `entrepreneurs / smoothed / the transition`。
  - `by dreaming up ways ...` 表方式；`dream up` = 想出、构思。
  - `to combine labor and machines` 修饰 `ways`，说明结合劳动与机器的办法。

  **③ The U.S. needs the new companies that will invent them.**

  - `that will invent them` 为限定性定语从句，修饰 `the new companies`。
  - `them` 回指上一句 `The best uses of 3D printers and virtual reality`，即新技术的创新用途，而不是打印机本身。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="第六、七段｜because 因果、四谓语并列与 find + 宾补">

  **① Finally, because automation threatens to widen the gap between capital income and labor income, taxes and the safety net will have to be rethought.**

  - 原因状语从句：`because automation threatens to widen the gap ...`。
  - 主句被动：`taxes and the safety net / will have to be rethought`；`safety net` 在政策语境指**社会保障兜底体系**。
  - `capital income` 是资本所得，`labor income` 是劳动所得，两者对照是全文分配政策建议的依据。

  **② Taxes on low-wage labor need to be cut, and wage subsidies such as the earned income tax credit should be expanded: This would boost incomes, encourage work, reward companies for job creation, and reduce inequality.**

  - 冒号前是两个并列的被动政策：`Taxes ... need to be cut` 与 `wage subsidies ... should be expanded`。
  - `such as the earned income tax credit` 举例说明针对劳动收入的抵免/补贴机制，并非一般意义的退还全部所得税。
  - 冒号后 `This would ...` 的 `This` 概括**两项政策的配套实施**；四个并列的动词 `boost`、`encourage`、`reward`、`reduce` 共享 `would`。

  **③ Technology will improve society in ways big and small over the next few years, yet this will be little comfort to those who find their lives and careers upended by automation.**

  - **并列主干：** `Technology / will improve / society`；`this / will be / little comfort`。
  - `who find their lives and careers upended by automation` 是定语从句修饰 `those`；其中 `find + 宾语 + 过去分词宾补` 表“发现自己的生活与事业已被……颠覆”。
  - `little comfort`（几乎无慰藉）与 `a little comfort`（有一点慰藉）含义不同。

</Accordion>

## Reading Text 1 补充：自动化冲击与政策回应

原文以“自动化造成阶段性就业冲击”为问题，以教育、创业和分配政策为解决路径。最后两句概括作者态度：**反对破坏技术，但主张主动帮助劳动者适应技术进步。**

**① Destroying the machines that are coming for our jobs would be nuts.**  
译：毁掉那些可能抢走我们工作的机器，是荒唐的做法。

- **主干：** `Destroying the machines / would be / nuts`。动名词短语 `Destroying ...` 整体作主语。
- **修饰：** `that are coming for our jobs` 为限定性定语从句，修饰 `machines`；`come for` 此处带拟人色彩，表示“来夺走（工作）”。
- **表达：** `nuts` 作表语形容词，口语中表示“疯狂的、荒唐的”，不是名词“坚果”。

**② But policies to help workers adapt will be indispensable.**  
译：但帮助劳动者适应变化的政策必不可少。

- **主干：** `policies / will be / indispensable`。
- **修饰：** `to help workers adapt` 为不定式短语作后置定语，说明政策的用途；`help sb. (to) do sth.` 中 `to` 可省略。
- **观点：** 作者拒绝“砸毁机器”，并不等于反对政府干预；`But` 明确转向对劳动者的政策支持。

<Callout type="idea" title="主题迁移：技术进步类阅读的判断框架">

阅读自动化、人工智能或产业变革议题时，先区分 **技术的长期社会收益**、**特定劳动者的短期成本** 和 **政策调节的必要性**。同时肯定技术创新与转型政策并不矛盾，不能将作者立场简化为“完全支持”或“完全反对”。

</Callout>

### Text 2 · 长难句与高频语法

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="01｜The implication is that ... prefer ... to be filtered（表语从句与宾补）">

  **句子主干：** `The implication / is / that ...`，`that` 引导表语从句，说明调查结果的深层含义。

  **从句内部：** `Millennials / prefer / news ... / to be filtered through other sources`，可按 `prefer + 宾语 + to do` 理解；`to be filtered` 为不定式被动式，表示“新闻被其他来源筛选或转述”。

  **易错：** `not a president's social media platform` 与 `other sources` 相对，不能译为“总统在平台上不发布新闻”。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="02｜Yet as distrust has risen ... people may be starting ...（as 的双重语义）">

  **主干：** `people / may be starting / to beef up their media literacy skills`。

  `as distrust has risen toward all media` 表示“随着媒体不信任感加深”，既含时间推移，也隐含原因。此处不是方式从句的“像……那样”。

  `may be starting` 表推测中的新趋势；`beef up` 是“加强”，不是字面“牛肉”。`media literacy` 指辨别来源、判断可信度和理解传播机制的综合能力。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="03｜During the 2016 presidential campaign ...（长主语与过去分词后置定语）">

  **压缩主干：** `nearly a quarter of web content / was / fake news`。

  - `shared by Twitter users ...`：过去分词短语修饰 `web content`，即“被用户分享的网络内容”。
  - `in the politically critical state of Michigan`：说明 `Twitter users` 所在地区；`of Michigan` 指明该州名称。
  - `according to the University of Oxford`：交代统计来源，并非作者亲自核验的普遍事实。

  **数字范围：** “近四分之一”针对特定竞选时期、地区和平台的分享内容，不能扩展为“全美国四分之一的新闻都是假的”。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="04｜A survey ... found 44 percent ... rarely or never trust ...（宾语从句省略 that）">

  **主干：** `a survey / found / (that) 44 percent of Facebook users rarely or never trust news ...`。

  `conducted for BuzzFeed News` 修饰 `survey`，不是 `users`；`rarely or never` 修饰动词 `trust`。`from the media giant` 中 `the media giant` 回指 Facebook。

  **翻译提示：** 此处是“受访者对脸书平台新闻的信任程度”，不能把“44%”错译为“44% 的人从不使用脸书”。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="05｜Young people who are digital natives ...（定语从句与分离搭配）">

  **主干：** `Young people / are becoming / more skillful`。

  `who are digital natives` 修饰 `Young people`；`at separating fact from fiction in cyberspace` 补充说明“擅长什么”。

  **关键结构：** `separate A from B` = 把 A 与 B 区分开。`fact` 和 `fiction` 构成对照，不应将 `from fiction` 单独理解为“虚构的来源”。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="06｜They cross-check sources and prefer news ... especially those ...（指代与透明度）">

  **并列谓语：** `They / cross-check / sources`；`(They) / prefer / news from different perspectives`。

  `those that are open about any bias` 中 `those` 指可比较的新闻来源或报道（联系前文的 `sources`），定语从句 `that are open about any bias` 说明这些来源**坦诚披露自身偏见**，不是“欢迎各种偏见”。

  `especially` 引出偏好中特别重要的一类，不能把它误读成全体消息来源的共同属性。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="07｜Social media allows ... while also permitting ...（并列谓语与省略）">

  **主干：** `Social media / allows / users / to experience ...`，`while (it is) also permitting / them / to re-share ...`。

  `while also permitting ...` 是省略结构，增加另一种同时存在的作用，不要机械理解为对立的“然而”。`as a projection of their values and interests` 表示用户通过转发表达价值观与兴趣，修饰转发这一行为。

  `allow/permit sb. to do sth.` 都要求不定式宾补；`be conscious of` 意为“意识到”。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="08｜A survey ... found the top reason ... is reader error（多层后置修饰）">

  **主干：** `A survey / found / (that) the top reason ... is “reader error”`。

  `given by Americans` 是过去分词后置定语，修饰 `reason`；`for the fake news phenomenon` 指“对假新闻现象所给出的成因解释”。`more so than ...` 是对比强调：相比捏造报道、事实错误，受访者更常认为读者误读是问题关键。

  **翻译原则：** 先译调查结论，再补原因比较，避免把 `reader error` 误读为“新闻编辑的工作失误”。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="09｜This indicates ... says Roxanne Stone（引语后置与宾语从句）">

  引述部分主干是 `This / indicates / (that) there is a real personal responsibility ...`。`in counteracting this problem` 说明责任所涉及的方面。

  报道语 `says Roxanne Stone` 为**引语后置的主谓倒装**，`editor in chief at Barna Group` 是主语的同位语；`editor in chief` 意为“主编”。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="10｜when young people are critical ... they reveal ...（时间从句与平行结构）">

  **主句：** `they / reveal / a mental discipline`；`when young people are critical ...` 是时间状语从句。

  `in thinking skills — and in their choices on when to share on social media` 为两组并列介词短语；`when to share` 是“疑问词 + 不定式”，作介词 `on` 的宾语。

  **立场辨析：** `be critical of` = 对……持批评态度，不必带“全面否定”之义；`mental discipline` 在此是**审慎、自我约束**，不是“精神惩罚”。

</Accordion>
</Accordions>

### Text 3 · 长难句逐层精讲

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="01｜Any fair-minded assessment ... must start by acknowledging ...（超长主语）">

  **主干：** `Any fair-minded assessment / must start / by acknowledging ...`。

  **主语的多级限定：** `assessment of the dangers of the deal between Britain's NHS and DeepMind`，按“评估 → 风险 → 协议 → 签约双方”逐层向外扩展。`by acknowledging` 表示方法，后接 `that both sides mean well` 宾语从句。

  **词义：** `mean well` = 怀有善意、出于好意，不能译成“意思表达得好”。句意是**先承认善意，再评价风险**，不等于免除责任。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="02｜The potential ... is very great, but ...（过去分词后置定语和双重评价）">

  **并列主干：** `The potential / is / very great`；`it / could lead to / further concentration of power`。

  `applied to healthcare` 是过去分词短语，修饰 `this work`；`of this work applied to healthcare` 整体说明 `potential` 的内容。`in the tech giants` 说明权力集中于科技巨头之中。

  **逻辑词：** `but` 后的风险是作者展开后文批评的关键，不能只记住前半句“潜力巨大”。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="03｜It is against that background that ... which ... which ...（强调句嵌套两级定从）">

  **第一层：强调背景。** `It is against that background that + 主谓宾` 是强调句；去掉 `It is ... that` 后，核心结构是：

  `Against that background, the information commissioner, Elizabeth Denham, has issued her damning verdict against the Royal Free hospital trust under the NHS.`

  **第二层：补充机构行为。** `which handed over to DeepMind the records of 1.6 million patients ...` 是非限制性定语从句，先行词为 `the Royal Free hospital trust`。原句发生宾语后移：`handed over the records ... to DeepMind`，使长宾语更易阅读。

  **第三层：说明协议缺陷。** `which took far too little account of the patients' rights ...` 是限定性定语从句，先行词为 `a vague agreement`。

  **术语纠错：** `damning verdict` 这里是监管机构**严厉、谴责性的裁断或评价**，不宜译成刑事意义的“定罪裁决”。`take account of` = 考虑到；`far too little` = 远远不够。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="04｜Further arrangements—and there may be many—... will be scrutinised（插入语隔开主谓）">

  **抽出主干：** `Further arrangements / will be scrutinised`。

  - `—and there may be many—`：插入说明今后的安排可能很多。
  - `between the NHS and DeepMind`：修饰 `arrangements`。
  - `to ensure ...`：不定式表目的。
  - `that all necessary permissions have been asked of patients and all unnecessary data has been cleaned`：`ensure` 后的一个 `that` 宾语从句，内部包含两组并列被动分句，而不是两个互不相关的宾语从句。

  **阅读技巧：** 遇到破折号先暂时跳过其中信息，找到 `arrangements` 和 `will be scrutinised`，再回填内容。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="05｜Ms Denham chose to concentrate the blame ... since ...（法律主体与原因状语）">

  **主句：** `Ms Denham / chose / to concentrate the blame on the NHS trust`。

  `since` 表原因而非“自从”。原因从句内部并列对比：`it “controlled” the data` 与 `DeepMind merely “processed” it`；`it` 指 `the NHS trust`，末尾 `it` 指数据。

  **关键词：** `concentrate the blame on` = 把责任集中归于……；`merely` = 仅仅，只是，带有弱化作用。引号是对法律意义上的“控制/处理”进行区分。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="06｜But this distinction misses the point that it is ... that ...（同位语从句内部再强调）">

  **外层主干：** `This distinction / misses / the point`。

  **同位语从句：** `that ...` 解释 `the point` 的具体内容；不是用于限定某一个“点”的普通定语从句。

  **内层强调句：** `it is processing and aggregation, not the mere possession of bits, that gives the data value`。

  **还原：** `Processing and aggregation, not the mere possession of bits, give the data value.` 英语原句使用 `gives`，在强调句中把 `processing and aggregation` 作为一个整体概念理解。

  **核心对照：** `processing and aggregation`（处理、汇集）与 `mere possession`（仅仅持有）对比；价值创造环节才是作者关注的权力来源。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="07｜The great question is who ... data that our lives ...（表语从句套定语从句）">

  **主句：** `The great question / is / who should benefit ...`，`who` 引导表语从句。

  **表语从句：** `who / should benefit / from the analysis of all the data ...`。

  **嵌套定语：** `that our lives now generate` 修饰 `data`，其中 `that` 在从句中作 `generate` 的宾语。

  **关键问法：** `who should benefit` 询问**应当受益的主体**，不是“数据如何被获取”，也不是“是否有利润”。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="08｜The data ... gains its value only when ...（only when 限定条件）">

  **主干：** `The data of an individual / gains / its value`。

  **条件限定：** `only when it is compared with the data of countless millions more`，表示“只有在与无数其他人的数据对比时才形成其价值”。这里 `when` 兼具条件意义。

  **结构提醒：** `only when` 位于句末，因此主句**不倒装**；若置于句首，通常写作 `Only when ... does the data gain ...`。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="09｜It is not enough to say that the algorithms ...（形式主语与省略关系代词）">

  **外层：** `It / is / not enough / to say ...`；`It` 是形式主语，真正主语为不定式短语 `to say ...`。

  **宾语从句：** `that the algorithms DeepMind develops will benefit patients and save lives`。

  `DeepMind develops` 前省略 `that/which`，构成限定性定语从句，修饰 `algorithms`。`will benefit patients and save lives` 为并列谓语结构。

  **立场：** 作者并没有断言算法无法救人，而是认为**仅以医疗收益为理由不足以回应权利与归属问题**。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="10｜What matters is that they will belong ... which developed them ...（主从、表从、定从）">

  **主语从句：** `What matters` = “真正重要的事情”。

  **表语从句：** `that they will belong to a private monopoly ...`，`they` 指 `the algorithms`。

  **定语从句：** `which developed them using public resources` 修饰 `a private monopoly`；`using public resources` 是现在分词短语表方式，强调开发过程中使用了公共资源。

  **核心冲突：** 公共资源参与开发 ↔ 算法成果可能归私人垄断企业所有。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="11｜If software promises ... on the scale that drugs now can（比较与省略）">

  **条件从句：** `If software promises to save lives on the scale that drugs now can`。

  `that drugs now can` 修饰 `the scale`；`can` 后承前省略 `save lives`。`on the scale` 表“达到……规模”，不是“天平上的药物”。

  **主句：** `big data / may be expected / to behave as big pharma has done`。`as big pharma has done` 是方式/类比从句，`has done` 承前代替相关行为。

  **理解重点：** 这是一种规范性类比：若软件能达到与药物相似的社会影响规模，大数据产业也应接受相应的期待与约束；不表示软件本身是药品。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="12｜small choices now may turn out ... / A long struggle ...（结果、目的与隐喻）">

  `turn out to do` = 结果是、最终证明会……；`may turn out to have gigantic consequences` 带推测，不能写成“必然导致巨大灾难”。

  `A long struggle will be needed to avoid a future of digital feudalism` 是被动主句加不定式目的状语；`digital feudalism` 是借用“封建等级与依附关系”形容数字权力高度集中，**不是作者声称现代社会已恢复历史上的封建制度**。

  `Ms Denham's report is a welcome start` 表示报告有积极意义，但仅是开始，不等于问题已被全面解决。

</Accordion>
</Accordions>

### Text 4 · 长难句逐层精讲

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="01｜It reported a net loss ... the 10th straight year ...（同位语和时间从句）">

  **主干：** `It / reported / a net loss of $5.6 billion`，`for fiscal 2016` 说明财年。

  `the 10th straight year ...` 是对 2016 财年所在连续亏损序列的补充说明；`its expenses have exceeded revenue` 中，省略了用于连接时间的关系词，可理解为“连续第十年支出超过收入”。

  **单位换算：** `$5.6 billion` = **56 亿美元**；`$120 billion` = **1200 亿美元**。阅读考试中特别注意英语 `billion` 和汉语“亿”的差距。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="02｜There are many reasons this institution finds itself ...（原因关系从句）">

  **主干：** `There are / many reasons`。

  `this formerly stable federal institution finds itself on the verge of bankruptcy` 补充说明 `reasons`，相当于 `reasons why ...`。

  `find oneself + 介词短语/形容词短语` = “发现自己处于某种境地”；`on the verge of bankruptcy` = 濒临破产。`formerly` 说明“过去稳定、如今陷入困境”的对照。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="03｜Fundamentally, the USPS is in a historic squeeze between A and B（超长并列后置定语）">

  **核心骨架：** `the USPS / is / in a historic squeeze between A and B`。

  **A：** `technological change that has permanently decreased demand for its bread-and-butter product, first-class mail`。

  - `that ... decreased demand` 修饰 `technological change`。
  - `first-class mail` 是 `bread-and-butter product` 的同位语，指其传统核心产品。

  **B：** `a regulatory structure that denies management the flexibility to adjust its operations to the new reality`。

  - `that denies management ...` 修饰 `regulatory structure`。
  - `deny sb. sth.` 是双宾语结构，“不给某人某物”。
  - `to adjust its operations` 修饰 `flexibility`；`to the new reality` 表调整的目标。

  **核心技巧：** 长句先抓 `between A and B`，再分别分析两个 `that` 定语从句，便能看清“两面夹击”的因果链。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="04｜interest groups ... exert self-interested pressure ... insisting that ...（现在分词和让步从句）">

  **主干：** `interest groups / exert / self-interested pressure on Congress`。

  - `ranging from postal unions to greeting-card makers` 修饰 `interest groups`，列举涉及范围。
  - `—Congress—` 是 `the USPS's ultimate overseer` 的同位语。
  - `insisting that ...` 是现在分词短语，说明这些利益集团施压时所坚持的要求。
  - `whatever else happens to the Postal Service` 是让步从句，“不论邮政署再发生什么变化”。
  - `aspects of the status quo they depend on get protected` 是 `insisting` 后的宾语从句；`they depend on` 是省略关系词的定语从句，修饰 `the status quo`。

  **误区：** `status quo` = 现状；`get protected` 是“获得保护”的被动含义，并非利益集团保护邮政署的所有利益。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="05｜This is why ... have failed, leaving the Postal Service ...（表语从句与结果状语）">

  **主句：** `This / is / why ...`，`why` 引导表语从句。

  **表语从句核心：** `repeated attempts at reform legislation / have failed`。

  **结果：** `leaving the Postal Service unable to pay its bills except by deferring vital modernization` 是现在分词短语作结果状语；`leave + 宾语 + 形容词宾补` 表“使某人/某机构陷入某状态”。

  `except by doing` 表唯一剩下的方式：**只能通过推迟现代化开支来维持支付**，不能译成“停止支付所有账单”。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="06｜Now comes word that everyone involved ... has agreed ...（地点/时间副词引起的倒装）">

  **表层：** `Now comes word that ...`；可改写为 `Word now comes that ...` 或 “Now there is news that ...”。

  **真正主语：** `word`（消息）；谓语为 `comes`。`that everyone involved ... has finally agreed ...` 是解释消息内容的**同位语从句**。

  **插入列举：** `—Democrats, Republicans, the Postal Service, the unions and the system's heaviest users—` 补充说明 `everyone involved`；由于中心语是 `everyone`，后接 `has finally agreed` 单数形式。

  `a plan to fix the system`：不定式修饰 `plan`，表计划要达成的目的。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="07｜Legislation is moving through the House that would save USPS ...（被后移的定语从句）">

  **主干：** `Legislation / is moving / through the House`。

  `that would save USPS an estimated $28.6 billion over five years` **回指句首的 Legislation**，虽然被状语 `is moving through the House` 与先行词隔开。

  `which could help pay for new vehicles ...` 为非限制性定语从句，说明这笔预计可节约的资金或相应节约效果可能用来支付新车辆等开支。

  **量级：** `$28.6 billion` = **286 亿美元**；`over five years` 是五年合计，不能读成“每年 286 亿”。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="08｜Most of the money would come from A and from B（并列资金来源）">

  **主干：** `Most of the money / would come / from A and from B`。

  **A：** `a penny-per-letter permanent rate increase`，每封信邮资永久增加一美分。

  **B：** `shifting postal retirees into Medicare`，将邮政退休人员转入 Medicare 体系；`shifting` 是动名词，作介词 `from` 的宾语。

  **后一句：** `The latter step` 指 B，不是 A；`offset the financial burden` 为“抵消/减轻财务负担”；`thus addressing ...` 表示由此带来的结果。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="09｜If it clears the House ... the Senate—where ...（条件、定从与宾语从句）">

  **条件：** `If it clears the House` = 如果法案获得众议院通过；`clear` 在立法语境是“通过一道审议程序”。

  **主句：** `this measure / would still have to get through / the Senate`。

  **补充说明：** `—where someone is bound to point out ...` 是非限制性定语从句，`where` 指参议院这一立法机构/审议场所。

  **宾语从句：** `that it amounts to the bare, bare minimum necessary to keep the Postal Service afloat, not comprehensive reform`。

  **抓对比：** `the bare, bare minimum` = 再低不过的最低限度；`afloat` = 尚能运转；`not comprehensive reform` = 不是全面改革。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="10｜There's no change ..., a major omission considering that ...（同位语与原因）">

  **主干：** `There is / no change to collective bargaining`。

  `a major omission` 是对“没有调整集体谈判机制”这一事实的补充评价；`considering that personnel accounts for 80 percent of the agency's costs` 表示评价依据。

  `account for` 此处意为“占……比例”；`personnel` 指人事/人员及其成本，不是“个人（personal）”。**80% 为人员成本占总成本的比例**。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="11｜Also missing is any discussion of ...（表语前置的全部倒装）">

  **原句：** `Also missing is any discussion of eliminating Saturday letter delivery.`

  **正常语序：** `Any discussion of eliminating Saturday letter delivery is also missing.`

  - `Also missing`（表语）置于句首，后面的 `is`（系动词）出现在主语 `any discussion ...` 之前。
  - `of eliminating ...` 修饰 `discussion`，`of` 后使用动名词。
  - `That common-sense change` 回指**取消周六信件投递**，而不是前文的 Medicare 调整。

</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="12｜The emerging consensus ... is a sign that ... / It is not ...（同位语从句与结论转折）">

  **两句镜像结构：**

  1. `The emerging consensus ... / is / a sign that legislators are getting frightened ...`。
  2. `It / is not / a sign that they're getting serious about transforming ...`。

  `that ...` 分别为 `a sign` 的**同位语从句**，说明是“何种迹象”；`get frightened about` 与 `get serious about` 是评价方向的对照。

  `however` 切换到更重要的否定判断。作者认为共识体现对**短期政治风险**的恐惧，而不是对**长期邮政改革**的认真承诺。

</Accordion>
</Accordions>

### Text 4 · 高频词、政策概念与数字归纳

**数据定位提示：**

- `2016 fiscal year`：净亏损 **56 亿美元**（历史事实，为文章写作背景）。
- `10th straight year`：连续 **第十年** 收不抵支。
- `more than $120 billion`：超过 **1200 亿美元** 的未落实资金准备的负债。
- `an estimated $28.6 billion over five years`：预计 **五年内合计**节约 286 亿美元，属于待实施改革的预测，不能当成已实现节约。
- `a penny per letter`：每封信上涨 **1 美分**，不是一美元。
- `80 percent`：人员成本在机构成本中的占比。
- `$2 billion per year`：取消周六信件投递**预计每年节省 20 亿美元**，这是当时讨论过但未纳入该法案的政策方向，不是已实现的收入。

<Callout type="idea" title="阅读技巧：识别‘救急’与‘治本’的论证差别">

这篇文章四个阶段分别回答：**为何亏损？为何改革失败？眼前有哪些措施？这些措施为何不够？** 最后的 `is a sign ... / is not ... a sign ...` 是最直接的作者态度证据。题目中出现 `full reform`、`long-term transformation`、`financial relief` 时，要先辨认是既定事实、提案预测还是作者评价。

</Callout>

## 阅读理解 Part B：艾森豪威尔行政办公楼的建筑与历史

### 阅读主题与衔接

本部分围绕美国政治与建筑史交汇处的 **Eisenhower Executive Office Building（EEOB，艾森豪威尔行政办公楼）** 展开。文章从历史文化地位切入，依次介绍设计方案、建造原因、侧翼竣工及内部装饰，最后说明它所见证的重要人物和历史事件。

**① The Eisenhower Executive Office Building (EEOB) commands a unique position in both the national history and the architectural heritage of the United States.**  
译：艾森豪威尔行政办公楼（EEOB）在美国的国家历史与建筑遗产中均占据独特地位。

- **主干：** `The Eisenhower Executive Office Building / commands / a unique position`。
- **限定：** `in both the national history and the architectural heritage of the United States` 补充其地位所属的两个领域；`both A and B` 强调双重身份。
- **词义：** `command` 在此并非“指挥”，而是“拥有（影响力、地位等）”；`architectural heritage` = 建筑遗产。

**② Designed by Supervising Architect of the Treasury, Alfred B. Mullett, it was built from 1871 to 1888 to house the growing staffs of the State, War, and Navy Departments, and is considered one of the best examples of French Second Empire architecture in the country.**  
译：这座建筑由美国财政部主管建筑师阿尔弗雷德·B. 穆莱特设计，于 1871 年至 1888 年间建成，旨在容纳美国国务院、战争部和海军部不断扩充的工作人员；它被认为是美国最具代表性的法兰西第二帝国式建筑之一。

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="长难句拆解：过去分词状语 + 两个被动谓语 + 目的状语">

  **先找主干：** `it / was built ... and is considered ...`。主语 `it` 指 EEOB；`was built` 与 `is considered` 是由 `and` 连接的两组被动谓语，分别说建造过程和历史评价。

  **句首设计信息：** `Designed by Supervising Architect of the Treasury, Alfred B. Mullett` 为过去分词短语作状语，逻辑主语是 `it`；人名是 `Supervising Architect of the Treasury` 的同位语。

  **时间：** `from 1871 to 1888` 修饰 `was built`。

  **用途：** `to house the growing staffs of the State, War, and Navy Departments` 为不定式目的状语；这里 `house` 是及物动词，表示“容纳”，不是名词“房子”。

  **评价：** `one of the best examples of French Second Empire architecture in the country` 是 `is considered` 后的主语补足语，指“一流典范之一”，而不是“唯一典范”。

  **专名辨析：** `State, War, and Navy Departments` 指当时美国的政府部门名称；`War Department` 是历史机构名，不宜套用现代部门名称造成时代错位。

</Accordion>
</Accordions>

### 第二段：行政机构与外交事务

**① The State, War, and Navy Building, as it was originally known, housed the three Executive Branch Departments most intimately associated with formulating and conducting the nation's foreign policy in the last quarter of the nineteenth century and the first quarter of the twentieth century—the period when the United States emerged as an international power.**

译：这座建筑最初名为“国务院、战争部与海军部大楼”，在 19 世纪最后 25 年及 20 世纪最初 25 年，曾容纳三个与美国外交政策的制定和执行最密切相关的行政部门。美国正是在这一时期崛起为国际强国。

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="长难句｜插入语、过去分词后置定语与时间定语从句">

  **主干：** `The State, War, and Navy Building / housed / the three Executive Branch Departments`。

  - `as it was originally known` 是插入性的补充说明，表示“按最初的名称”；`as` 在此可理解为“正如……”。
  - `most intimately associated with ...` 是过去分词短语，后置修饰 `the three Executive Branch Departments`。`associated with` 后的 `formulating` 和 `conducting` 是两个并列动名词，分别指“制定”和“执行”。
  - `in the last quarter ... and the first quarter ...` 限定这三个部门发挥作用的历史阶段。
  - 破折号后的 `the period` 对前面的时段作同位补充；`when the United States emerged ...` 为修饰 `the period` 的定语从句。

  **翻译技巧：** 英语中的长后置定语可前移为中文定语，也可拆成独立句；不要将 `formulating` 与 `conducting` 都译作“制定”。

</Accordion>
</Accordions>

**② The building has housed some of the nation's most significant diplomats and politicians and has been the scene of many historic events.**

译：这座建筑曾为美国一些极具影响力的外交官与政治家提供办公场所，也见证了许多历史事件。

- **主干：** `The building / has housed ... / and has been ...`。现在完成时强调与建筑历史地位相关的经历。
- **词义：** `house` 是动词“容纳、为……提供场所”；`the scene of` 表示“……的发生地”，不可按“场景照片”机械翻译。

### 第三段：旧办公楼、火灾与拆迁

**① The history of the EEOB began long before its foundations were laid.**

译：早在这座建筑奠基之前，它背后的历史便已经开始。

- **主干：** `The history of the EEOB / began`。
- **时间关系：** `long before its foundations were laid` 是 `before` 引导的时间从句；`were laid` 为一般过去时被动语态，`lay the foundations` 意为“奠基”。

**② The first executive offices were constructed between 1799 and 1820.**

译：最早的一批行政办公建筑建于 1799 年至 1820 年间。

**③ A series of fires (including those set by the British in 1814) and overcrowded conditions led to the construction of the existing Treasury Building.**

译：接连发生的火灾（包括 1814 年英军引发的火灾）及过度拥挤的办公条件，促成了现存财政部大楼的兴建。

- **主干：** `A series of fires and overcrowded conditions / led to / the construction ...`；两个因素共同构成原因。
- **定语：** `those set by the British in 1814` 中 `those` 代指火灾，`set by ...` 为省略 `that were` 的被动定语结构。
- **搭配：** `lead to + 名词/动名词` 表示“导致、促成”，不是“领导前往”。

**④ In 1866, the construction of the North Wing of the Treasury Building necessitated the demolition of the State Department building.**

译：1866 年，财政部大楼北翼的建设使拆除原国务院办公楼成为必要。

- **主干：** `the construction ... / necessitated / the demolition ...`。
- **辨析：** `necessitate` = “使……成为必需”；它说明工程之间的因果关联，不宜误译为“批准拆除”。

### 第四段：选址、方案与建筑风格

**① In December of 1869, Congress appointed a commission to select a site and prepare plans and cost estimates for a new State Department Building.**

译：1869 年 12 月，国会任命一个委员会，负责为新的国务院办公楼选择地点、拟订方案并编制费用估算。

- **主干：** `Congress / appointed / a commission`。
- **任务：** `to select ... and prepare ...` 表示委员会被任命后的职责，两个动词共享不定式标记 `to`。
- **词组：** `cost estimates` = “费用估算”；`site` 在此为“建设地点、地块”。

**② The commission was also to consider possible arrangements for the War and Navy Departments.**

译：该委员会还需考虑战争部和海军部的办公安置方案。

- **结构：** `was to consider` 为 `be to do`，表示计划、安排或职责；不是“已经考虑”。

**③ To the horror of some who expected a Greek Revival twin of the Treasury Building to be erected on the other side of the White House, the elaborate French Second Empire style design by Alfred Mullett was selected, and construction of a building to house all three departments began in June of 1871.**

译：令一些人震惊的是，他们原本期待在白宫另一侧建起一座与财政部大楼相呼应的希腊复兴式建筑，但最终入选的却是阿尔弗雷德·B. 穆莱特精心设计的法兰西第二帝国式方案；1871 年 6 月，这座为三个部门共同提供办公场所的大楼正式动工。

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="重点｜两个主句 + 一个嵌套定语从句">

  **先略过句首：** `the elaborate ... design / was selected, and construction ... / began` 是由 `and` 连接的两组并列主谓结构。

  **再分析句首：** `To the horror of some` 表示“令一些人震惊的是”，其中 `who expected ...` 修饰 `some`（一些人）。

  **定语从句内部：** `expected / a Greek Revival twin ... / to be erected ...`，为 `expect + 宾语 + to do` 的被动不定式形式，意思是“期待某物被建造”。

  **末尾不定式：** `a building to house all three departments` 中 `to house ...` 修饰 `a building`，说明建筑用途；不是 `construction began` 的目的状语。

  **态度线索：** `To the horror of some` 反映部分人的反对或意外，不等于作者本人否定该建筑风格。

</Accordion>
</Accordions>

### 第五段：建造历程与内部设计

**① Construction took 17 years as the building slowly rose wing by wing.**

译：随着大楼一翼接一翼地逐步建成，整个施工过程持续了 17 年。

- `as` 引导伴随时间推移的状语从句；`wing by wing` 是“逐翼、一个侧翼接一个侧翼”，与施工分阶段推进相呼应。

**② When the EEOB was finished, it was the largest office building in Washington, with nearly 2 miles of black and white tiled corridors.**

译：EEOB 竣工时是华盛顿规模最大的办公楼，楼内黑白瓷砖铺设的走廊总长近两英里。

- `When ... was finished` 是时间状语从句；`with nearly 2 miles of ... corridors` 补充建筑特征。**原文使用复数 `corridors`**，不是“一条近两英里长的走廊”。

**③ Almost all of the interior detail is of cast iron or plaster; the use of wood was minimized to insure fire safety.**

译：几乎所有室内装饰构件都采用铸铁或灰泥，并尽量减少木材的使用，以确保消防安全。

- **并列关系：** 分号连接两个独立分句，第二分句进一步说明防火考量。
- **目的：** `to insure fire safety` 为不定式目的状语；此处 `insure` 按语境作“确保”，而非“购买保险”。

**④ Eight monumental curving staircases of granite with over 4,000 individually cast bronze balusters are capped by four skylight domes and two stained glass rotundas.**

译：八座宏伟的花岗岩弧形楼梯配有 4,000 多根分别铸造的青铜栏杆柱，上部与四座采光穹顶和两处彩色玻璃圆形大厅相衔接。

- **主干：** `Eight ... staircases / are capped by / four ... domes and two ... rotundas`。
- **修饰：** `with over 4,000 ... balusters` 修饰 `staircases`；`individually cast` 修饰 `bronze balusters`。
- **词义：** `cap` 作动词表示“覆盖、置于顶部”；`stained glass` 是“彩色玻璃”，不是“有污渍的玻璃”。

### 第六段：部门迁入与装饰细节

**① Completed in 1875, the State Department's south wing was the first to be occupied, with its elegant four-story library (completed in 1876), Diplomatic Reception Room, and Secretary's office decorated with carved wood, Oriental rugs, and stenciled wall patterns.**

译：国务院所在的南翼于 1875 年建成，是最早投入使用的部分；其中有典雅的四层图书馆（1876 年建成）、外交接待室，以及用木雕、东方地毯和模板印制的墙面图案装饰的国务卿办公室。

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="长难句｜分词状语 + 序数词后置不定式 + with 补充结构">

  - **主干：** `the State Department's south wing / was / the first to be occupied`。
  - **句首分词：** `Completed in 1875` 表示南翼建成的时间，逻辑主语是 `the ... south wing`。
  - **不定式：** `the first to be occupied` = “最早被投入使用的部分”；`to be occupied` 为被动形式。
  - **with 补充：** `with its ... library, ... Room, and ... office ...` 列举内部设施，宜在译文中另起分句。
  - **装饰成分：** `decorated with carved wood, Oriental rugs, and stenciled wall patterns` 主要描述 `Secretary's office` 的装饰，不能误判为“南翼由地毯建造”。

</Accordion>
</Accordions>

**② The Navy Department moved into the east wing in 1879, where elaborate wall and ceiling stenciling and marquetry floors decorated the office of the Secretary.**

译：1879 年，海军部迁入东翼；那里的海军部长办公室采用精致的墙面与天花板模板装饰，并铺设镶嵌工艺地板。

- **主干：** `The Navy Department / moved into / the east wing`。
- **定语从句：** `where ... decorated ...` 补充说明东翼这一地点；`where` 在从句中作地点状语。
- **主谓对应：** 从句主语是并列的 `wall and ceiling stenciling and marquetry floors`，谓语 `decorated`，宾语 `the office`。

### 第七段：重要人物与历史事件

**① Many of the most celebrated national figures have participated in historical events that have taken place within the EEOB's granite walls.**

译：许多美国著名人物都曾参与在这座花岗岩建筑内部发生的重大历史事件。

- `that have taken place ...` 为定语从句，修饰 `historical events`，不是修饰 `national figures`。

**② Theodore and Franklin D. Roosevelt, William Howard Taft, Dwight D. Eisenhower, Lyndon B. Johnson, Gerald Ford, and George H. W. Bush all had offices in this building before becoming president.**

译：西奥多·罗斯福、富兰克林·D. 罗斯福、威廉·霍华德·塔夫脱、德怀特·D. 艾森豪威尔、林登·B. 约翰逊、杰拉尔德·福特以及乔治·H. W. 布什，都曾在担任总统之前于此办公。

- `before becoming president` 是时间状语，主语由主句中的列举人物承接；人名虽多，**句子主干仅为 `... all had offices in this building`**。

**③ It has housed 16 Secretaries of the Navy, 21 Secretaries of War, and 24 Secretaries of State.**

译：这里先后为 16 位海军部长、21 位战争部长和 24 位国务卿提供过办公场所。

- 三个数字分别对应三个部长职务，不能将数字与部门错配。

**④ Winston Churchill once walked its corridors and Japanese emissaries met here with Secretary of State Cordell Hull after the bombing of Pearl Harbor.**

译：温斯顿·丘吉尔曾走过大楼的走廊；珍珠港遭袭之后，日本特使曾在这里与美国国务卿科德尔·赫尔会面。

- **并列句：** `Winston Churchill / walked ...` 与 `Japanese emissaries / met ...`。
- **时间：** `after the bombing of Pearl Harbor` 直接限定后一个会面事件，不应扩展为丘吉尔参观的时间。
- **同位关系：** `Cordell Hull` 与 `Secretary of State` 指同一人。

## 翻译 Part C：莎士比亚时代的英国戏剧

### 主题线索：从宗教戏剧衰退到职业剧团兴起

文章回顾莎士比亚出生前后戏剧形式的变化：宗教剧逐渐式微，受古典文学影响的新式戏剧出现；职业演员、公共剧院、大学出身的剧作家共同推动英国戏剧发展。翻译时应优先识别**并列、时间和因果**，而非被人名和戏剧分类打乱主干。

### 第一段：戏剧形式与职业化的出现

**① Shakespeare's lifetime was coincident with a period of extraordinary activity and achievement in the drama.**

译：莎士比亚的一生恰逢戏剧活动极为活跃、戏剧成就格外辉煌的时期。

- **主干：** `Shakespeare's lifetime / was coincident with / a period`。
- **词组：** `be coincident with` = “与……同时发生”；`in the drama` 表示“在戏剧领域”。

**②【翻译重点 · 原题 46】 By the date of his birth Europe was witnessing the passing of the religious drama, and the creation of new forms under the incentive of classical tragedy and comedy.**

译：到莎士比亚出生之时，欧洲正见证宗教戏剧的衰落，以及受古典悲剧与喜剧推动的新戏剧形式的诞生。

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="翻译题 46｜一个谓语与两个并列宾语">

  **主干：** `Europe / was witnessing / the passing ... and the creation ...`。

  - `By the date of his birth` 为时间状语；`his` 指莎士比亚。
  - `the passing of the religious drama` 与 `the creation of new forms` 是同一谓语 `was witnessing` 的并列宾语，分别表示“消亡”和“诞生”。
  - `under the incentive of classical tragedy and comedy` 修饰新形式的产生背景，可顺译为“在古典悲剧和喜剧的推动之下”。

  **易错：** `passing` 不能机械译为“经过”；`the creation` 不能漏译，否则因果与对照关系不完整。

</Accordion>
</Accordions>

**③ These new forms were at first mainly written by scholars and performed by amateurs, but in England, as everywhere else in western Europe, the growth of a class of professional actors was threatening to make the drama popular, whether it should be new or old, classical or medieval, literary or farcical.**

译：这些新式剧作起初主要由学者编写、业余演员演出；但在英格兰——和西欧其他地方一样——随着职业演员群体日渐壮大，戏剧正趋于走向大众，无论它是新式还是旧式、古典还是中世纪、文学性的还是滑稽性的。

- **转折前：** `These new forms / were ... written ... and performed ...`，两个被动动作并列。
- **转折后：** `the growth of a class of professional actors / was threatening / to make the drama popular`。`threaten to do` 在此表示“看来可能／即将”，不宜一律译成带负面色彩的“威胁”。
- **插入比较：** `as everywhere else in western Europe` 是“如同西欧其他各地一样”的比较性插入结构。
- **让步范围：** `whether it should be ...` 中的 `it` 指 `the drama`；后面列出三组并列对举，省略重复的 `whether it should be`。

**④【翻译重点 · 原题 47】 Court, school, organizations of amateurs, and the traveling actors were all rivals in supplying a widespread desire for dramatic entertainment; and no boy who went to a grammar school could be ignorant that the drama was a form of literature which gave glory to Greece and Rome and might yet bring honor to England.**

译：宫廷、学校、业余戏剧团体和巡回演员都在竞相满足人们对戏剧娱乐的广泛需求；而任何上过文法学校的男孩都不会不知道，戏剧是一种曾为希腊和罗马赢得荣耀、将来也可能为英格兰带来荣誉的文学形式。

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="翻译题 47｜分号并列 + 宾语从句内再套定语从句">

  **主干一：** `Court, school, organizations of amateurs, and the traveling actors / were / all rivals`。`in supplying ...` 说明竞争的方面：满足娱乐需求。

  **主干二：** `no boy ... / could be ignorant / that ...`。`who went to a grammar school` 是修饰 `boy` 的定语从句。

  **宾语从句：** `that the drama was a form of literature ...` 解释男孩所知晓的内容。

  **嵌套定语从句：** `which gave glory to Greece and Rome and might yet bring honor to England` 修饰 `a form of literature`，内部两个谓语 `gave` 与 `might ... bring` 并列。

  **否定理解：** `no boy ... could be ignorant` 表示“没有哪个男孩不知道”，在中文中可转换为“每个……都知道”，切忌译为“所有男孩都无知”。

</Accordion>
</Accordions>

### 第二段：公共剧院和文学戏剧的融合

**① When Shakespeare was twelve years old the first public playhouse was built in London.**

译：莎士比亚十二岁那年，伦敦建成了第一座公共剧院。

**② For a time literature showed no interest in this public stage.**

译：在一段时间里，文学界对这一公共舞台并不感兴趣。

- `for a time` = “一度、曾有一段时间”，与 `for the time being`（暂时）区分。

**③ Plays aiming at literary distinction were written for schools or court, or for the choir boys of St. Paul's and the royal chapel, who, however, gave plays in public as well as at court.**

译：追求文学艺术水准的剧作原本主要写给学校、宫廷，或圣保罗大教堂和皇家礼拜堂的唱诗班男童；不过，这些男童既在宫廷演出，也在公共场所登台。

- `aiming at literary distinction` 是现在分词后置定语，修饰 `Plays`；`literary distinction` 侧重“文学特色／艺术卓越性”。
- `who, however, gave plays ...` 为非限制性定语从句，`who` 的先行词是 `the choir boys`，`however` 是插入语。
- `as well as` 在此连接两个地点短语，表示“两者都”，不能误读成比较程度。

**④【翻译重点 · 原题 48】 But the professional companies prospered in their permanent theaters, and university men with literary ambitions were quick to turn to these theaters as offering a means of livelihood.**

译：然而，专业剧团在固定剧院中兴旺起来，而那些怀有文学抱负的大学出身者也很快转向这些剧院，把它们视为谋生的途径。

- **第一分句：** `the professional companies / prospered / in their permanent theaters`。
- **第二分句：** `university men with literary ambitions / were quick / to turn to these theaters ...`。
- **识别身份：** `university men` 在历史语境中是受过大学教育的人，不必限缩为“在校大学生”。
- **含义：** `turn to ... as offering ...` 表示“转向……，认为其能够提供……”。`livelihood` = “生计、谋生手段”。

**⑤ By the time that Shakespeare was twenty-five, Lyly, Peele, and Greene had made comedies that were at once popular and literary; Kyd had written a tragedy that crowded the pit; and Marlowe had brought poetry and genius to triumph on the common stage—where they had played no part since the death of Euripides.**

译：到莎士比亚二十五岁时，利利、皮尔和格林已创作出既通俗又具有文学性的喜剧；基德已写出能令剧院观众席爆满的悲剧；马洛则使诗意与天才在公共舞台上大放异彩——自欧里庇得斯去世以来，它们还未在这样的舞台上发挥过作用。

- `By the time ...` 与三组过去完成时 `had made / had written / had brought` 呼应，强调“到那一时点已发生”。
- `at once popular and literary` 表示“既受欢迎又具文学性”，**不是**“曾一度受欢迎”；`at once` 此处相当于 `both`。
- `that crowded the pit` 修饰 `a tragedy`；`the pit` 在历史剧院语境下指观众区域，`crowd` 是“挤满”。
- `where they had played no part ...` 中 `where` 指公共舞台，`they` 指 `poetry and genius`。

**⑥【翻译重点 · 原题 49】 A native literary drama had been created, its alliance with the public playhouses established, and at least some of its great traditions had been begun.**

译：具有本土特色的文学戏剧已经形成，它与公共剧院的联系也已确立，其伟大传统中至少有一部分已开始奠基。

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="翻译题 49｜独立主格结构，不是三个完整的并列句">

  - **完整主句一：** `A native literary drama / had been created`。
  - **中间的独立主格：** `its alliance with the public playhouses established` = `its alliance ... having been established`，由“名词 + 过去分词”构成，补充相关状态。原句此处**没有**完整的限定动词 `had`，不宜误标为独立并列句。
  - **完整主句二：** `and at least some of its great traditions / had been begun`。

  **翻译顺序：** 本土戏剧形成 → 与公众剧场建立联系 → 传统逐渐确立。中文可补出“也已”，保持叙述流畅。

</Accordion>
</Accordions>

### 第三段：伊丽莎白时代戏剧的繁荣与散佚

**① The development of the Elizabethan drama for the next twenty-five years is of exceptional interest to students of literary history, for in this brief period we may trace the beginning, growth, blossoming, and decay of many kinds of plays, and of many great careers.**

译：此后二十五年伊丽莎白时代戏剧的发展，对文学史研究者具有非同寻常的价值；因为在这一短暂时期中，人们可以追溯各种剧种及许多杰出戏剧家事业的兴起、成长、鼎盛与衰落。

- `be of exceptional interest to` = `interest ... exceptionally`，可译为“对……极具研究价值”。
- 逗号后的 `for` 作并列连词，引出原因，不能理解为“为了”。
- `beginning, growth, blossoming, and decay` 形成四阶段时间序列，应保留完整顺序。

**② We are amazed today at the mere number of plays produced, as well as by the number of dramatists writing at the same time for this London of two hundred thousand inhabitants.**

译：今天，仅当时创作的戏剧作品数量就足以使我们惊讶；更不用说同时为只有二十万人口的伦敦写作的剧作家人数了。

- `plays produced` = `plays that were produced`，过去分词短语修饰 `plays`。
- `dramatists writing ...` 是现在分词短语修饰 `dramatists`。
- `the mere number` 强调“仅仅数量本身就……”，不是“单纯的数字”。

**③【翻译重点 · 原题 50】 To realize how great was the dramatic activity, we must remember further that hosts of plays have been lost, and that probably there is no author of note whose entire work has survived.**

译：要充分认识当时戏剧活动有多么繁荣，我们还必须记住：大量剧作已经散佚，而且很可能没有任何一位知名作家的全部作品完整保存至今。

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="翻译题 50｜程度从句、双宾语从句与 whose 定语从句">

  **目的状语：** `To realize how great was the dramatic activity`，其中 `how great was the dramatic activity` 是 `realize` 所接的程度内容。原句沿用了较为文学化的倒装形式，阅读时恢复为 `how great the dramatic activity was` 便于理解。

  **主干：** `we / must remember / that ... and that ...`。

  **第一宾语从句：** `that hosts of plays have been lost`；`hosts of` 表示“大量”，`have been lost` 为现在完成时被动语态。

  **第二宾语从句：** `that probably there is no author of note ...`；`of note` = “知名的、重要的”。

  **嵌套定语：** `whose entire work has survived` 修饰 `author`；`whose` 表示所属关系，不能译成“谁”。

  **理解核心：** 保存至今的剧作并不等于当年的全部创作，作者是在提醒读者：对当时戏剧繁荣程度的判断还应计入已散佚的作品。

</Accordion>
</Accordions>

### Part C · 核心词汇与翻译模板

| 词语 / 搭配 | 准确释义 | 易错提醒 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `the passing of` | ……的消逝、衰退 | 不等于“通过考试” |
| `under the incentive of` | 在……的激励或推动下 | 保留因果关系 |
| `threaten to do` | 看起来即将、可能会 | 语境不一定消极 |
| `professional company` | 专业剧团 | `company` 不总是商业公司 |
| `give plays` | 演出戏剧 | `give` 不宜逐字直译 |
| `at once A and B` | 既 A 又 B | 此处不是“立刻” |
| `crowd the pit` | 使剧场观众区挤满人 | `pit` 有特定历史语义 |
| `be of exceptional interest` | 具有特别的研究价值 | `of + 抽象名词` 常表性质 |
| `hosts of` | 大量的 | 数量很大，不是“主人” |
| `author of note` | 著名作家 | `of note` 表示值得注意 |
